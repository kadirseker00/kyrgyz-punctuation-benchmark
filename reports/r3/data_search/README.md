# Kyrgyz speech-data search — 4 November 2026

The requested use case is **audio plus independently supplied human text containing punctuation**, in Kyrgyz Cyrillic, preferably spontaneous speech, dialogue, or long recordings. An ASR-generated transcript is not an independent punctuation reference. This search did not establish that no suitable corpus exists worldwide.

| Candidate and primary source | Finding | Decision for this revision |
|---|---|---|
| [Google FLEURS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) | Public CC-BY-4.0; `ky_kg` test has 977 recordings and a punctuated `raw_transcription` field. Explicitly read speech. Revision `70bb2e84b976b7e960aa89f1c648e09c59f894dd`. | Evaluate the complete test split; adds another source and longer sentences, but cannot satisfy spontaneous/dialogue coverage. |
| [Common Voice Spontaneous Speech 5.0 metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/common-voice/cv-dataset/65ca2460d5e686ea03902a932bbca09b322dc4b0/datasets/spontaneous-speech/sps-corpus-5.0-2026-09-11.json) | 80 locales; `ky` is absent. Metadata and its hash are saved beside this note. Scripted and spontaneous releases must be distinguished. | No Kyrgyz spontaneous split in this inspected release. Existing Common Voice 21.0 **scripted** Kyrgyz test remains usable for the expanded read-speech evaluation. |
| [AUCA Analyzing Kyrgyz Narratives (AKYN)](https://www.auca.kg/en/auca_news/4896/), [archive catalog](https://catalog.archivum.org/catalog/O8pZgyZa?tab=context), [transcriptions](https://www.auca.kg/en/research_faculty_akyn_transcriptions/) | Six human-transcribed oral epic performances by two performers, around six hours. The archive allows access but states that third-party reproduction rights require clearance. The inspected first Bakchiev and Sydykov transcripts contain punctuated Kyrgyz Cyrillic; they are line-based rather than supplied as timestamped ASR test segments. Metrical oral epic differs from everyday conversation. | A promising candidate for a separate long oral-narrative study, **not an established open-license conversational benchmark**. Audio/transcript correspondence, segmentation and reuse rights remain to be established. No recordings redistributed, rights inferred, or evaluations claimed. |
| [Helsinki HALS spoken Kyrgyz project](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/423235112/yilandiloglu_hals_2024.pdf) | Presentation describes 40 minutes of fieldwork speech and manual transcript correction. It describes release as future work; no downloadable audio-plus-punctuation corpus was identified in the inspected source. | A research lead; no usable release verified. |
| [M2ASR-Kirghiz paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/14/1/55) | 128 hours, 163 speakers; prompted reading in Xinjiang. Script, access, and usable original punctuation require separate checks. | Does not add spontaneous speech; not used as an independent punctuation reference. |
| [KSTU Kyrgyz ASR-Verified v1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kstunlp/kyrgyz-asr-verified-v1/blob/main/README.md) | Card restricts access/use to KSTU members or affiliates and forbids outside redistribution. | Not a generally accessible benchmark for this study. No access request sent. |
| [GigaAM Multilingual](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10371v1), [authors' repository](https://github.com/salute-developers/GigaAM) | Paper includes Kyrgyz in an **internal** spontaneous-speech evaluation. The inspected release does not provide those test recordings and references. | Evidence of an internal benchmark does not provide usable open evaluation data. |

Other Hugging Face leads were inspected (`Speech-data/Kyrgyz-Speech-Dataset`, `samil24/kyrgyz-asr`, `shunyalabs/kyrgyz-speech-dataset`, `averoo/kyrgyz_mono`, wake-word feature datasets). Their inspected cards/files did not establish an independently human-punctuated spontaneous Kyrgyz benchmark with suitable audio and clear reuse terms. A single sample, undocumented re-upload, text-only corpus, or wake-word features cannot substitute for that evidence.

AKYN text inspection: [Bakchiev transcription 1](https://auca.kg/en/research_faculty_akyn_transcriptions_bakchiev_transcription1/) and [Sydykov transcription 1](https://auca.kg/en/research_faculty_akyn_transcriptions_sydykov_transcription1/). Both credit Alymkan Jeenbekova for transcription. The Bakchiev page explicitly identifies an initial spoken introduction and links the audiovisual item through a persistent handle; this is a useful lead for a future manual alignment audit, not a verified dialogue split.

**Outcome:** expanded evaluation uses two read-speech sources. Spontaneous dialogue and genuinely long natural recordings remain untested. No provider was contacted, no paid/gated access obtained, and no permission to redistribute restricted material assumed.
